2026 Amazon FBA Fee Guide & Margin Defense

While Amazon US remains the largest e-commerce marketplace globally, nuanced and tiered fee models often mask hidden deficits. Mastering fulfillment tiers and advertising economics is essential to scaling sustainable gross margins.

1. Understanding Referral Fees

Amazon charges a percentage on every unit sold, typically 15% for major consumer categories (Beauty, Home, Kitchen, Electronics). Certain niches like apparel feature sliding scales from 8% to 17% depending on the total sale price threshold.

2. FBA Fulfillment Tiers (Dimensional Weight)

FBA fees depend on package dimensions and unit shipping weight. Even a fractional inch difference can push your package from Standard to Large Oversize, adding $2.00 to $4.00 per unit in fulfillment surcharges. Always optimize product packaging before shipping container volume to Amazon fulfillment centers.

3. Break-Even ROAS Calculation Formula

Your break-even ACoS (Advertising Cost of Sales) equals your gross margin before advertising. If your net margin before PPC is 35%, spending above 35% on PPC results in negative cashflow. Convert this to ROAS using 100 / Margin% to evaluate PPC campaigns in real time.

How the FBA profit calculator works

The profit on one sale is the selling price minus your product cost, Amazon's referral fee, fulfillment and shipping cost, and ad spend.

Referral fee = price × 15% (FBA / FBM) or price × 2.9% + $0.30 (Shopify)

Net profit = price − product cost − referral fee − fulfillment/shipping − ad spend

Break-even ROAS = price ÷ (price − total cost before ads) × 100

Worked example

With a $30.00 price, $8.50 product cost, $5.40 fulfillment and shipping, and no ad spend, the referral fee is $4.50 and the net profit is 30.00 − 8.50 − 4.50 − 5.40 = $11.60, a margin of about 38.7%. Costs before ads total $18.40, so you can spend up to $11.60 on ads; break-even ROAS is 30 ÷ 11.60 × 100 = about 259%.

Things to know

  • The 15% referral rate is an example value. Amazon's referral rates differ by category, and some are lower or higher. Check your category in Seller Central.
  • Fulfillment fees depend on size and weight, so you enter them yourself.
  • Storage fees, returns, taxes and currency changes are not included.

Results are estimates to support your own judgment.
