Understanding US Paycheck Withholding and State Tax Burdens (2026)

Your gross salary rarely matches the net deposit in your checking account. United States payroll withholding is divided into three distinct layers: Federal Income Tax, Federal Insurance Contributions Act (FICA), and State & Local taxes.

1. 2026 Federal Tax Brackets and Standard Deduction

The US uses a progressive tax bracket system (10%, 12%, 22%, 24%, 32%, 35%, 37%). For a single filer with a $100,000 salary, your effective federal tax rate lands between 14% and 16% after the standard deduction.

2. Mandatory FICA Deductions (7.65%)

Every employee must contribute to Social Security and Medicare:

  • Social Security: 6.2% on income up to the statutory annual wage limit.
  • Medicare: 1.45% on all earnings with no cap (an additional 0.9% surtax applies to high earners).
  • Combined: A fixed 7.65% mandatory payroll deduction.

3. The Financial Impact of State Relocation

Living in a state with zero income tax (like Texas or Florida) saves a $150,000 earner over $12,000 annually compared to high-tax jurisdictions like California or New York, significantly boosting disposable income.
