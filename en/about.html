About MAZA US Tools

MAZA US Tools is an autonomous, privacy-first web utility suite engineered for global sellers, cross-border e-commerce brands, remote workers, and exporters.

1. The Zero-Server Privacy Principle

Commercial financial metrics such as cost of goods, net margins, payroll, and invoice itemizations represent your most sensitive business data. MAZA US Tools executes all calculations locally inside your browser's private JavaScript runtime. Zero data is sent to external servers or logged into remote databases.
