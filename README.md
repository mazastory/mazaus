## 🛠️ Features
- **Amazon FBA Net Profit Calculator:** Real-time 2026 referral fees, size tiers, and break-even ROAS.
- **US Paycheck Salary Tax Calculator:** 50-state tax withholding (Federal, FICA, State).
- **Stripe & PayPal Merchant Fee Calculator:** Card processing, cross-border margins, and reverse invoice invoicing.
- **Unit & Size Converter:** Inches/cm, lbs/kg, °F/°C, and US/KR/EU shoe & clothing sizing.
- **Commercial Export Invoice Generator:** Pure browser-based export invoice with instant PDF print.
